FAQ

Common Questions

What makes this different from just hiring someone full-time?

A full-time hire costs more, takes months to ramp, and locks you into a headcount commitment regardless of how much AI work you actually have month to month. A fractional engagement scales with the work.

How long until I see results?

The Assess engagement is time-boxed and fast — typically weeks, not months. Build & Adopt timelines depend on scope, but every case study above resulted in something running, not just a recommendation.

What industries do you work with?

Manufacturing, retail, financial services, and insurance are where I've delivered the deepest results, but the pattern — a mid-market company that's outgrown ad hoc tools but doesn't need a full internal AI team — applies broadly.

What if you recommend I don't need AI for this?

That's a legitimate outcome. I'm not incentivized to sell you a build you don't need — the goal is the right call, not a bigger invoice.

Do you work with private equity firms and portfolio companies?

Yes — I'm available to work with PE firms and operating partners on portfolio-company engagements, though my primary focus is working directly with mid-market operating companies.

How do we start?

An Assess engagement is the lowest-friction way in — it's scoped, time-boxed, and gives both of us a clear answer on whether a bigger engagement makes sense.
